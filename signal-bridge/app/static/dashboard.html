TV→bridge uses TradingView's alert time; bridge→EA is the round trip until the EA reported the result.

Slippage vs TradingView compares the MT5 fill with the price in the alert, which comes from TradingView's data feed, not your broker's quotes. It includes the difference between the two feeds as well as real execution slippage. "Broker slippage" compares the fill with the price MT5 quoted when the order was sent. Expected PnL prices the same trades at TradingView's prices; the gap to actual PnL is the total cost of execution.

Runs only change what the dashboard shows by default: signals and deals are never deleted, and "All time" always shows everything. A trade belongs to the run it was opened in.
